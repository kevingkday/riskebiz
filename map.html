Trade Risk Map

A world view for exporters and foreign investors, showing where political risk insurance and trade credit insurance are useful.

Market data loads from the files on this site.

  • Pins are places where political risk and/or trade credit cover is especially relevant, based on public classifications and published reporting.
  • OECD country-risk categories, when shown, come from the official OECD Country Risk Classification and measure official export-credit country risk: transfer and convertibility risk, and force-majeure political events. They are not Riskebiz's own score.
  • This is educational information, not a recommendation that a specific country is unsafe or that cover is available.
  • Operators, exporters, or brokers can inquire about cover through the contact form.
How the pins are chosen

Countries in prevailing OECD categories 5, 6, and 7 on the list valid as of 16 October 2026 are pinned. High-income participants the OECD leaves unclassified are not given a number here. Categories 1 to 4 are not pinned. OECD classification · Current PDF

Further reading, other houses' own views: Coface, Allianz Trade, Credendo. Their scores are not used on this map.
